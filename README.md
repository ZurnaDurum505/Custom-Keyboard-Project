# Custom-Keyboard-Project 
9/9/2026
I started my keyboard by starting to make the PCB using Kicad. To do the PCB I had to do the schematics first by placing switches for the keys and adding diodes on every switch so the keyboard wont start "ghosting" when multiple keys are pressed. Then i added stabilizers for each key that was going to be larger than 2U. After placing everything I started wiring the keyboard making rows and columns so i can wire them to the Pico micro controller faster, and to finish it off i added my mounting holes at the corners of my design. After that I just assigned footprints and annotated my schematics. Also if you struggle with planning out your keyboard i recommend using this website https://www.keyboard-layout-editor.com/#/ It lets you design your own keyboard by placing keys and adjusting the how many U's you want your keys to be.

After the schematics I moved to the pcb editor where draw the outline of my pcb and  reorganized my keys, diode, and stabs inside the bcb outline(because it doesn’t come to you organized in the pcb editor). After everything looked how i wanted to order to be, i placed the pico inside the b b outline with its USB port facing outside and slightly outside of my pcb outline before I started firing the pcb. I wired my keys as columns on the front board and my diode as rows on the back board, making sure to not make any of the wires in the same board side touch eachother. After the wiring was done I started making a case for it on in shape CAD. I started by making a bottom plate slightly bigger than the pcb, them making walls that are around 10-15 mm tall. Before I did the top plate, I scratched 14mm to 14mm squares on every spot a key needed to go (and also sketch holes for your stabs I eyeballed the size for mine). After that was done I made the top plate around 3mm thick closing on the pcb and extrude removed the spaces the keyholes would be from my scetch, creating holes for my keys. Before you move onto customizing yourdesign make a 5 to 10mm hole on where your pico’s port would be. After that is up to you to make it look how you want, I added another layer not covering a rectangle area where keys would be and extrude removed to carve my logo.
